# GavixaCare — Business Model

## Revenue Streams

### 1. B2C Freemium (primary consumer revenue)

| Tier | Price | Key features |
|---|---|---|
| Free | ₹0 | Hospital compare, 3 AI bill audits / month, emergency finder, basic vault (5 docs) |
| Individual Premium | **₹29/mo** or **₹249/yr** | Unlimited AI audits + estimates, full vault, priority AI, saved analyses |
| Family Premium | **₹79/mo** or **₹699/yr** | Everything above + up to 6 family profiles + shared vault |
| Professional (doctors/nurses) | **₹199/mo** | Patient lookup, referral tools, professional analytics, verified badge |

**Target Year 1:** 1M free users → 5% conversion = 50,000 paid → **~₹2.45 Cr ARR** at blended ₹41/mo ARPU.

### 2. Hospital SaaS (B2B)

| Tier | Price | Features |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | **₹3,999/mo** | Basic listing, 1 seat, standard badge |
| Growth | **₹9,999/mo** | Verified badge, analytics, review management, 3 seats |
| Enterprise | **₹24,999/mo** | Full dashboard, bed availability API, custom reporting, dedicated CSM |

**Target Year 2:** 500 hospitals × ₹8,000 avg = **₹48 Cr ARR** at scale.

### 3. Insurance Partner API
- Fraud detection per-query: **₹2–5**
- Fair-rate benchmark per-query: **₹1–3**
- Aggregate market intelligence subscription: **₹5–20L/report** or **₹50L+/yr license**

### 4. Government Contracts (B2G)
- District health dashboard: **₹50L–2 Cr / state / year**
- Bed-shortage prediction & analytics as SaaS to NHA / state health departments

### 5. Diagnostic Labs & Pharmacy
- Lab test booking commissions: **8–12%**
- Medicine referrals: **3–5%** of order value

### 6. Healthcare Data Intelligence (with explicit consent, DPDP-compliant)
- Anonymised market reports for pharma / device makers / health economists

### 7. Health Insurance Lead Generation
- ₹500–2,000 per converted policy

## Unit Economics (target)

| Metric | 6-month | 12-month |
|---|---|---|
| CAC (organic + partnerships) | ₹40 | ₹60 |
| ARPU (blended premium) | ₹41 / mo | ₹45 / mo |
| Contribution margin | 55% | 65% |
| Payback period | 3–4 months | 2 months |
| LTV / CAC | 8× | 12× |

## Fundraising Plan

### Angel / Pre-seed (immediate)
- **Amount:** ₹50L – ₹75L
- **Use:** ABDM certification, legal setup, first 3 hires, brand IP filing.

### Seed round (Month 6–12)
- **Amount:** **₹6 – ₹8 Cr** (~$720K–960K)
- **Runway:** 18 months for 8-person team + AI infra + city ops.
- **Split of use:**
  - Engineering & AI: 35%
  - Hospital onboarding & data ops: 25%
  - Legal & compliance (ABDM + DPDP): 15%
  - Growth: 15%
  - Ops/Admin: 10%
- **Target investors:** Nexus, Blume, Elevation, Chiratae, Kalaari, Stellaris; strategic angels from Apollo, Manipal, insurance execs.

### Series A (Month 18–24)
- **Amount:** **₹30 – ₹50 Cr**
- **Milestones required:** 500K MAU, ₹3 Cr ARR, 3 state MoUs, insurance API live.

## Go-to-Market Sequencing

1. **Month 1–3 (Delhi NCR):** 500 verified hospitals; 50K MAU; PR moment via anonymised bill-fraud report.
2. **Month 3–6 (Tier 1 expansion):** Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad; iOS + Android launch; first 50 paying hospitals.
3. **Month 6–12 (Tier 2 + rural):** Pune, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Lucknow, Patna, Bhopal; WhatsApp bot becomes primary interface for rural users; first state MoU.
4. **Year 2:** national coverage + international pilot (Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, Nepal).

## Growth Loops

- **Bill audit → share result card** → friend downloads app → they audit their own bill.
- **Doctor ambassador** → doctors recommend to patients → verified professional tier drives B2C acquisition.
- **Media loop** → curated anonymised data × healthcare journalists → free PR → organic growth.
- **Hospital transparency badge** → hospitals voluntarily publish pricing to earn the badge → data moat grows.
